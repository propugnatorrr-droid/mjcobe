# **MJ COBE WEBSITE — MASTER PRODUCT SCOPE**

## **1\. Core concept**

Every MJ COBE song gets its own living campaign page.

That page becomes the permanent history of that record:

* song preview  
* cover art  
* release status  
* campaign goal  
* money contributed  
* number of supporters  
* business sponsors  
* fan leaderboard  
* campaign updates  
* social content  
* official video  
* streaming links after release  
* behind-the-scenes content  
* milestones  
* permanent supporter history

So instead of somebody simply donating $50 and disappearing, they become part of that song’s documented journey.

The website should make people feel:

**“I helped this record happen.”**

# **32\. Campaign allocation**

Because you’re not promising investment returns, you don’t need to track every dollar publicly unless you want to.

But transparency can be useful.

Example:

### **WHAT THIS CAMPAIGN SUPPORTS**

45% — Marketing

25% — Visuals

15% — Content

10% — Promotion

5% — Artist Development

I would avoid guaranteeing exact allocation unless you actually intend to account for it rigidly.

Instead say:

Campaign support helps fund marketing, visual production, content, promotion and the continued development of MJ COBE.

That gives you flexibility.

**luxury music editorial × streaming platform × cultural movement.**

Large imagery.

Minimal text.

Big typography.
