# MJ COBE — PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS

Authoritative product scope. Visual law lives in `DESIGN.md`.
Engine rules (ranking, ledger, state machines) live in `ENGINE.md`.

## 1. Core concept

Every MJ COBE song gets its own living campaign page. That page becomes the
permanent history of that record: song preview, cover art, release status,
campaign goal, money contributed, number of supporters, business sponsors, fan
leaderboard, campaign updates, social content, official video, streaming links
after release, behind-the-scenes content, milestones, permanent supporter history.

Instead of somebody donating $50 and disappearing, they become part of that
song's documented journey.

The website should make people feel: **"I helped this record happen."**

## 2. Main navigation

Public navigation stays extremely simple:

HOME | MUSIC | BACK A SONG | JOURNEY | PARTNERS | MJ COBE

And one strong button: **BACK A RECORD** — which remains highly visible on mobile.

## 3. Homepage

Must immediately establish MJ as an artist, not as somebody asking for money.

Hero: large cinematic MJ image/video.

> **MJ COBE**
> SOUL HAS A NEW FACE.
> Original R&B. A new visual world. A career being built in real time.
> [LISTEN] [BACK A RECORD]

Below: **Currently Building** — large featured song card.

> CAN'T READ YOUR MIND
> 73% FUNDED
> $18,420 raised toward $25,000 campaign goal
> 486 supporters
> [BACK THIS SONG]

Then:
- **#1 BUSINESS SPONSOR** — logo, company name, amount contributed
- **TOP FAN SUPPORTER** — name/username, amount

This introduces the ecosystem without overwhelming people.

## 4. Music page

MJ's actual catalog. Sections: **Released** (streaming links available),
**Coming Soon** (preview available), **Building Now** (accepting support),
**Vault** (optional later).

Each song card: cover, title, 15–30 second preview, status, supporter count,
funding percentage, button.

> CAN'T READ YOUR MIND — Coming Soon
> 486 people are helping build this record.
> $18,420 / $25,000
> [JOIN THE JOURNEY]

## 5. The most important page: SONG JOURNEY

Every song receives a unique URL: `mjcobe.com/song/cant-read-your-mind`
This is the product page.

Top section: large cover/video, title, MJ COBE, audio preview. Then $18,420
raised, 486 supporters, progress bar.

Campaign objective copy: "Help us fund the visual campaign, content, marketing
and rollout behind 'Can't Read Your Mind.'"

Buttons: **SUPPORT THIS SONG** and **SPONSOR THIS SONG**.
Keep fans and companies separate paths.

## 6. Fan support system

Fans support at predetermined amounts or enter their own.

$10 SUPPORTER / $25 DAY ONE / $50 INNER CIRCLE / $100 GOLD SUPPORTER /
$250 FOUNDING SUPPORTER / $500+ EXECUTIVE SUPPORTER / CUSTOM AMOUNT

Tier names configurable from Super Admin. Different songs can have different tiers.

## 7. What fans receive

Most benefits are digital/status based — physical merchandise fulfillment
destroys margins.

- **$10 Supporter** — name on supporter wall, campaign updates
- **$25 Day One** — above, plus Day One badge, early preview access
- **$50 Inner Circle** — above, plus private content, downloadable exclusive
  visual/artwork
- **$100 Gold Supporter** — priority placement on supporter wall, exclusive
  video messages/content, private livestream invitations
- **$250 Founding Supporter** — permanent Founding Supporter designation,
  premium profile placement, selected credits on campaign page, exclusive drops
- **$500+** — potentially signed/limited physical merchandise, private virtual
  listening session, name in visual credits, VIP event opportunities

Everything customizable.

## 8. Fan leaderboard

Fans choose **Display Name** or **Anonymous**. They may optionally link

## 9. The Outbid mechanic

Beside the #1 position:

> TAKE THE #1 SPOT
> Current #1: $1,250
> To take #1: $1,251+

Supporter enters amount. But nobody is forced into bidding — someone can still
simply give $20. This preserves both normal support and competitive attention.

## 10. Business sponsorship

Its own section, because businesses need a completely different experience.

Button: **SPONSOR THIS RECORD**

Business form: Business Name, Representative Name, Email, Phone, Website,
Instagram, Logo, Industry, Contribution Amount, Optional message →
[SUBMIT SPONSORSHIP]

Businesses must understand: sponsorship does not provide ownership of MJ COBE
or the underlying music. Sponsorship purchases promotional placement and
participation in the campaign.

## 11. Business sponsorship leaderboard

Visually premium. **OFFICIAL SONG PARTNERS**

> #1 PRESENTING PARTNER — [BRAND LOGO] — Company Name — $10,000 contributed — Visit Sponsor →

> #3 — $3,750

## 12. Business Outbid system

> CURRENT #1 SPONSOR — ABC Clothing — $7,500

updates. Minimum increments optional (current #1 $7,500 → minimum new bid
$8,000 rather than $7,501).

Super Admin controls: minimum bid, minimum increase, starting amount, whether
auto-approval is allowed.

## 14. Brand content opportunity

MJ being digitally generated is the leverage here. High-level packages can
include MJ x BRAND CONTENT — e.g. a clothing company backs a song, MJ is
generated wearing their clothing, and the sponsorship includes song-page
placement, an MJ branded social visual, a collaborative Instagram post, Story
placement, campaign acknowledgment, website click-through.

Premium tier, because it requires creative work.

- **DIGITAL PARTNER — $2,500** — site placement
- **FEATURED PARTNER — $5,000** — site + social acknowledgment
- **VISUAL PARTNER — $10,000** — MJ-created branded visual + site + social campaign
- **PRESENTING PARTNER — $25,000+** — premier campaign placement

All configurable.

Payments go directly to the MJ business account.

Every payment carries metadata: `user_id`, `song_id`, `campaign_id`,
`support_type`, `fan/business`, `tier`, `amount`, `display_name`,
`leaderboard_visibility`, `sponsor_status`.

**ADD TIER** — Tier Name, Price, Description, Benefits, Badge, Quantity Limit,

## 27. Leaderboard controls

Complete control. Per contribution: Name, Amount, Date, Song, Type, Rank,
Verified, Visible, Refunded.

Admin actions: APPROVE / HIDE / EDIT DISPLAY NAME / VERIFY / REFUND / BLOCK /
REMOVE FROM LEADERBOARD.

A refund automatically updates rankings.

## 28. Business verification

Business payments above a configurable amount enter **PENDING APPROVAL**.
Admin reviews company, website, social, representative, business email, logo,
intended promotion → **APPROVE SPONSOR** or **DECLINE + REFUND**.

## 29. Brand-safety controls

Admin blocklist covering business domains, email addresses, names, categories,
industries. Admin can designate prohibited sponsorship categories — industries
that shouldn't be associated with the artist.

## 30. Fan moderation

Admin approves what becomes public, or somebody pays $10 and makes their
display name something crazy.

Auto-approve normal usernames but flag URLs, profanity, political slogans,
adult content, hate speech, competitor advertising. Businesses always get
manual approval.

## 31. Refund controls

Select transaction → FULL REFUND or PARTIAL REFUND. Reason: Unverified Sponsor
/ Fraud Risk / Brand Safety / Duplicate Payment / Customer Request / Other.
Stripe refund issued, leaderboard recalculates automatically.

## 32. Campaign allocation

Optional transparency display:

> WHAT THIS CAMPAIGN SUPPORTS
> 45% Marketing / 25% Visuals / 15% Content / 10% Promotion / 5% Artist Development

Avoid guaranteeing exact allocation unless it will be accounted for rigidly.
Default copy: "Campaign support helps fund marketing, visual production,
content, promotion and the continued development of MJ COBE." That preserves
flexibility.

## 33. Terminology

Do NOT call these payments **donations**. MJ COBE is not a charity.
Use: BACK / SUPPORT / CONTRIBUTE / SPONSOR / JOIN THE JOURNEY.

This sentence must appear during checkout:

> Contributions are voluntary support for MJ COBE's creative and promotional
> campaigns and do not provide ownership, equity, royalties, securities,
> repayment rights or financial returns.

## 41. Super Admin permissions

## 42. Every major setting is editable

Never hard-code: prices, support tiers, campaign goals, language, colors, song
status, badges, sponsor levels, leaderboard size, minimum bid, outbid
increments, campaign dates, refund messages, confirmation text.

Sponsors, Sponsor Packages, Transactions, Refunds, Badges, Song Updates,

Keep songs and campaigns SEPARATE. One song may eventually have a release
campaign, music video campaign, remix campaign and tour campaign without
creating a new song object.

## 45. Visual direction

Do NOT look like Kickstarter. Do NOT look like GoFundMe. Do NOT look like a
fintech app.

Look like: **luxury music editorial × streaming platform × cultural movement.**

Mostly dark. Large imagery. Minimal text. Big typography. Cinematic MJ
photography. Subtle animation. Campaign numbers beautifully integrated. Support
mechanics feel almost secondary until someone enters a song page.

Full enforceable spec in `DESIGN.md`.

## 46. Psychological hierarchy

The visitor must experience, in order:

1. THIS ARTIST IS DOPE.
2. THIS SONG IS DOPE.
3. PEOPLE ARE ACTUALLY BACKING THIS.
4. I CAN BE PART OF IT.
5. WHERE DO I RANK?

Never: "Please give this artist money." That distinction makes or breaks the concept.

# APPENDIX A — SCOPE ADDITIONS

Additive only. Nothing above is removed. Detailed rules move to `ENGINE.md`
before Slice 3.

**Payments before Stripe.** A `PaymentProvider` interface with three
implementations: `mock` (realistic checkout UI, magic test numbers, fires the
same internal events Stripe would), `offline` (admin-only, for §38 wires), and
`stripe` (added at go-live). No business logic imports Stripe. Persistent
SIMULATION MODE ribbon and `is_test` flagging while mocked.

**Ledger-first accounting.** Append-only `ledger_entries`; all totals derived.
Transaction states: initiated → authorized → captured → settled, with failed,
canceled, refunded, partially_refunded, disputed, charged_back. Only `settled`
counts publicly. Makes §27 and §31 recalculation correct rather than aspirational.

**Ranking rules made explicit.** Rank unit is supporter-identity-per-campaign;
value is the SUM of settled contributions; ties break on who reached the amount
first. Every recompute writes a `rank_snapshot` — which yields "you held #1 for
nine days" as a badge, a share graphic and a journey event.

**Top-up to reclaim #1.** Outbid supporters pay only the delta plus the
increment, not a full re-bid. Higher conversion, and fairer.

**Anti-sniping window.** A #1 challenge inside the campaign's final 24h extends
that window, so the crown is won in public. Pending challenges display live.

**Spend guardrails.** Configurable 24h per-account ceiling, soft interstitial
above a weekly threshold, quiet hours and frequency caps on outbid
notifications, and opt-out from competitive alerts that does not remove someone
from the leaderboard.

**Entitlement delivery for §7.** `gated_assets`, `entitlement_grants`,
`asset_access_log`. Short-lived signed URLs, download counts, optional
per-user watermarking, and a `/vault` route that shows what you have and teases
what you don't.

**Audio protection.** Never serve the master. Generate derivatives on upload:
HLS + MP3 preview clipped to preview_start/preview_end, `peaks.json` waveform,
optional voice-tagged private preview for sponsor pitches. Full tracks behind
signed, entitlement-checked URLs.

**Audit and consent.** Immutable `audit_log` on every admin mutation.
Versioned `consent_records` storing the hash of the exact §33/§34 text agreed to.

**Sponsor commercial layer.** Sequential PDF invoices and receipts (businesses
cannot expense a payment-processor email — this is where $10k sponsorships
stall), sellable category exclusivity, generated sponsor contract PDFs,
properly measured impressions (50% visible for 1s, session-deduped) to feed the
§40 sponsor dashboard, and secret draft-preview links for pitching a campaign
page before it is public.

**Config surfaces.** `site_copy` (every string, DB-resolved with file
fallback), `settings`, `feature_flags` — so §42 is literally true.

**Momentum features (Phase 2/3).** Referral leaderboards, gift-a-backing,
"notify me when this song opens" waitlists with countdown, sponsor-matched
power hours, campaign-close countdowns, reserved supporter-number ranges.

**Visual canon for a digital artist.** A locked look book: one character sheet
(single face, defined age and build, max three hairstyles), a lens language, a
single grade LUT applied through one pipeline, wardrobe palette inside the site
palette, and grain plus slight motion blur added in post — pristine renders are
what read as synthetic. Generation metadata (model, seed, prompt, references)
stored in `lookbook_assets` for reproducibility, with admin approval before
anything goes public.

**Disclosure.** MJ's digitally-generated nature is stated openly and
confidently as part of the story, not buried. Brands doing due diligence will
find out; discovering it themselves converts the most interesting asset into a
scandal.
